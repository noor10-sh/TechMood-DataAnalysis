### Sales Analytics SQL & Business Insights Project

Welcome to the Sales Analytics Queries project! This project focuses on analyzing database performance, customer purchasing behavior, and product profitability using advanced SQL queries and actionable business insights.

## What I DO (SQL Implementation)

I utilized a comprehensive relational database consisting of three core tables: Customers, Products, and Sales. To answer key business questions, i implemented:

Multi-Table Joins (INNER JOIN & LEFT JOIN): Connected customers, products, and sales records to track purchasing trends and identify unengaged customers or unsold products.

Subqueries: Filtered high-value products where the unit price exceeded the overall average product price.

Aggregation Functions (SUM, COUNT, AVG with GROUP BY): Grouped data by categories, cities, and customer names to evaluate total revenue, transaction counts, and average quantities.

Comprehensive Analysis: Combined customer details, product categories, and individual product names to extract detailed revenue breakdowns.

## Key Business Insights

Based on SQL data queries, i extract the following strategic insights:

1. Premium Products Drive Revenue:

Filtering via subqueries proves that higher-priced items generate the majority of total revenue, reflecting strong customer willingness to invest in premium categories.

2. Category-Specific Growth:

Grouping sales by category highlights core revenue drivers, allowing the business to optimize inventory allocation.

3. Identification of VIP Customers:

Connecting customer names with total sales reveals top-tier buyers who contribute significantly to overall company revenue, opening doors for targeted loyalty programs.

4. Volume vs. Value Discrepancy:

An increase in total sales revenue does not always align with massive unit sales volume, indicating that pricing strategy plays a vital role in financial success.

5. Targeted Promotional Opportunities:

Cross-analyzing customers and product categories creates clear opportunities for cross-selling and running promotional offers to boost underperforming segments.
