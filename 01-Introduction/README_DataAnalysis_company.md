1. Starbucks Case Study
Starbucks Corporation is a global coffee company. For this case
study, the focus is on its financial and operating performance.
A suitable business problem identified for Starbucks is:
Starbucks experienced a significant decline in profitability in North
America during fiscal year 2025, even though revenue increased. The
analysis should investigate why profitability declined and identify
the factors contributing to the change.
Main Analytical Question:
Why did Starbucks' North America profitability decline in FY2025
despite an increase in revenue?
Supporting Questions:
Did revenue increase or decrease?
Did operating income increase or decrease?
How did operating margin change?
Did comparable-store sales change?
Did the number of comparable transactions change?
Did the average ticket change?
Did operating expenses increase?
How did restructuring and impairment costs affect profitability?

For answer all following questions the data Needed is:
Revenue, Operating income, Operating margin, Comparable-store sales, Comparable transactions, Average ticket
Store operating expenses, Restructuring and impairment expenses, Store count
A key analytical idea is to compare revenue growth with
profitability.
If revenue increases while operating income and operating margin
decrease, this indicates that higher revenue did not translate into
higher operating profitability.

Business Decision: Based on the analysis, Starbucks could focus on:
-Controlling operating costs.
-Improving store-level efficiency.
-Evaluating the financial impact of restructuring.
-Improving customer transactions.
-Monitoring whether strategic investments and store changes improve
profitability.

3. Main Learning from the Analysis:
The most important concept is that a business analysis is not simply
about collecting data or making charts.
A strong analysis connects:
- Questions
- Required Data
- Analysis
- Findings
- Decision
For Starbucks, the key idea is understanding why profitability can
decline even when revenue increases. For Careem, the key idea is understanding when and where demand is high
and using that information to improve driver availability and customer
service.

4. Final Conclusion
Both Starbucks and Careem demonstrate how data can support real business
decisions.
Starbucks: Financial and operational data can help the company understand changes
in revenue, profitability, customer transactions, expenses, and
restructuring effects. The results can support decisions aimed at
improving profitability and operational efficiency.
Careem: Ride and operational data can help the company understand customer
behavior, identify peak demand periods, identify high-demand locations,
and improve Captain distribution. The results can support decisions
aimed at reducing waiting times and improving the customer experience.

The overall purpose of Business Problem Analysis is therefore: Use data to understand a business problem, discover evidence-based, insights, and turn those insights into practical decisions.
